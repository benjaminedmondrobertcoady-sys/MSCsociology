Order Restored

Explore Émile Durkheim's functionalist view of society by investigating how institutions work together to maintain stability. Analyse evidence, identify social functions, and move through the four stages: Affirm, Clarify, Unify and Change.

Thinking skill: systems thinking & functional analysis Open ▸

Positive Deviants

Read fifteen newspaper profiles of people who broke the norm for the better, then file each under the right kind of positive deviant: altruism, charisma, innovation, supra-conformity, innate characteristics or the ex-deviant. Only your first placement counts, so read before you file.

Thinking skill: classifying & inferring from evidence Open ▸

Social Control Theory

Raise a life from childhood to adulthood and watch Travis Hirschi's four social bonds, attachment, commitment, involvement and belief, decide whether trouble takes hold. Choose from three very different people, predict what each will do at every turning point, and finish with a diagnosis rather than a score.

Thinking skill: applying theory & testing its limits Open ▸

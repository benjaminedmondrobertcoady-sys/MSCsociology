Key Words

  • preposition
    a word showing where, when, or how (on, under, near, by...)
  • prepositional phrase
    preposition + noun group (near the gate, on the slope)
  • location
    where something is
  • phrase
    a group of words — not a full sentence
  • detail
    extra information that helps the reader picture it
